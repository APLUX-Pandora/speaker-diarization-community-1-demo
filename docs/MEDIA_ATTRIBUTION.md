# Meeting audio attribution

The bundled sample uses segments of the AMI Meeting Corpus meeting EN2002a
(AMI Consortium), distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
It joins eight excerpts from the single distant microphone track with short
pauses and stores them as mono PCM. The excerpts contain four speakers.

[AMI Meeting Corpus](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/) ·
[AMI audio distribution](https://huggingface.co/datasets/edinburghcstr/ami)
