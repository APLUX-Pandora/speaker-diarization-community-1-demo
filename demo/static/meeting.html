Meeting details

During the meeting, Audio8 transcribes the audio being played. After ending, the complete captured recording is analyzed for speaker IDs. Final text retains the live transcript.

Word times and speaker labels are estimates. Ambiguous words and overlapping voices may remain unassigned. Speaker IDs are anonymous and local to this meeting; no audio source separation is performed.

Transcript association uses approximate ASR timestamps for both Chinese and English. Speaker turns come from the diarization model; text near a turn boundary may remain unassigned.

This file-playback meeting demo supports recordings up to 30 minutes. Audio8 streaming capability is separate from this demo's recording/storage limits.
